# Process Basics

1. What is Mentoring? 

Mentoring is an ever-evolving process that plays a vital role in shaping one's personal and professional growth. It is a personality-driven approach that recognizes and caters to an individual's specific requirements and ambitions for their life and career. The process involves frequent feedback and guidance, which is tailored to cater to the participant's distinct personality traits, strengths, and areas that require improvement. With Mentoring for Career Development, individuals receive the support and guidance required to achieve their career aspirations, providing them with the necessary tools and resources to not just succeed but be a leader in their domain of work.

2. What is Counselling? 

Counselling is a process of meaningful conversation between a licensed counsellor and an individual. It involves exploring challenges, identifying potential solutions, and developing the necessary mental and emotional tools for successful implementation. At Svastrino, our personalised counselling services are designed to help you navigate life's challenges with confidence and clarity.

3. How is counselling different from mentoring?

Counselling and Mentoring are both valuable tools to get support and guidance for personal and professional development. While Counselling is a short-term guidance program that helps individuals explore immediate problems and identify quick fix solutions, mentoring is a longer-term, personalised training program that helps individuals get guidance, advice, and support to drive results in a more comprehensive and focused way. Mentoring is tailored to each individual's unique needs and provides a detailed plan for achieving their career goals. At Svastrino, we offer both counselling and mentoring services, including personality-based mentoring programs that are personalised as per the individuals needs, to help our clients succeed in their life and careers.

4. How is Mentoring done?   
   

Mentoring is a personalised process that focuses on verifying and planning future steps for the application of an evolving, unique career plan. At Svastrino, we follow a structured approach to mentoring listed below

* Listen with patience: Our first step is to listen to you as a neutral mentor, with patience and without giving any suggestions or opinions at that point in time. We are here to listen and understand your situation.  
* Find the core issue: We work with you to identify the actual problem that needs to be addressed, rather than rushing towards a solution. We want to understand the core problem so we can help you find the best solution.  
* Help you accept the facts: We understand that it can be challenging to let go of preconceived notions and biases. We make sure you are comfortable and understand the situation neutrally. With this process, it becomes easier for you to build acceptance of the facts and move forward.  
* Identify your potential: We believe that everyone has great hidden potential that is yet to be found. With our Q\&A process, we try to unravel those facts and figures to guide you on further life, education and career possibilities.  
* Explore all options: Our Mentoring for life and career development facilitates the exploration of all possible options, their pros and cons, benefits, and situational possibilities. We help you understand every angle and then verify all the available options as per your current needs.

## Psychometric Testing:

1. What is Psychometric Testing?

Psychometric testing is a structured way of understanding different aspects of you, such as your interests, preferences, strengths, abilities and behavioural tendencies. It gives you an opportunity to discover yourself better and use that understanding while thinking about your academic and career choices.

2. Why should I take a Psychometric Test?

Choosing a stream or career only because of marks, trends or what others suggest may not always be the right approach. A psychometric test aims to give you greater self-understanding and direction, helping you look at your future choices from a more informed perspective.

3. Is Psychometric Testing only for students who are confused about their careers?

Not at all. Whether you are completely unsure, have a few options in mind or already have a career goal, the test can help you understand how your interests and characteristics relate to different possibilities and give you another perspective to consider.

4. At what age should a student take a Psychometric Test?

Different tests are useful at different stages of a student's development. Svastrino offers assessments designed around specific stages, including stream exploration and career exploration, so that the insights remain relevant to where you are in your journey.

5. Which Psychometric Tests are available on Svastrino?

Svastrino plans to offer different assessments based on the student's stage and requirement. These include assessments designed to help with stream selection and career exploration, allowing you to choose the assessment that is most relevant to your current decision.

6. How does a Psychometric Test work?

You will answer a series of carefully structured questions about yourself. There are no answers that you need to memorise or prepare for. Your responses are analysed to identify meaningful patterns and generate insights that can help you understand yourself and explore suitable possibilities.

7. Are there right or wrong answers in a Psychometric Test?

No. A psychometric assessment is not a school examination. There is generally no “correct” answer that you are expected to choose. The most useful results come when you answer honestly and naturally, based on what genuinely reflects you.

8. What does the Psychometric Test measure?

Depending on the assessment you take, it can explore areas such as interests, preferences, abilities, personality or behavioural tendencies. These insights are then used to help you understand yourself and explore academic or career possibilities that may align with your profile.

9. Will the test tell me exactly what career I should choose?

A psychometric test is not designed to make the decision for you. Instead, it aims to narrow the possibilities and provide meaningful direction based on your profile, giving you a stronger starting point for exploring careers that may suit you.

10. How many career options will I get from the test?

The number of career possibilities can vary depending on your assessment results and profile. Rather than simply giving you a long list of careers, the aim is to help you identify relevant possibilities worth exploring and understand why they may be suitable for you.

11. Can the test tell me which stream I should choose after Class 10?

A stream-selection assessment can help you understand which academic streams may be more aligned with your interests, abilities and profile. It is intended to support your decision—not replace your own thinking, conversations with parents or guidance from educators and counsellors.

12. What will I receive after completing the test?

You will receive a structured report based on your responses, presenting relevant insights about your profile and potential areas for exploration. The report is designed to help you understand the “why” behind the recommendations, rather than simply giving you an answer.

13. How should I use my Psychometric Test report?

Think of the report as a starting point for exploration, not a final verdict. You can use the insights to research careers, compare possibilities, discuss options with your parents or counsellor and decide what you would like to explore further.

14. Can my parents take the test on my behalf?

The assessment is designed around your own responses, so the student should complete it personally. Parents can support the process, but the most meaningful insights come when the student answers honestly based on their own thoughts and preferences.

15. Should I prepare before taking the test?

No special preparation is required. In fact, preparing for particular answers may make the results less representative of you. Simply take some time, understand each question and answer honestly based on what feels most natural to you.

16. How long does the Psychometric Test take?

The time can vary depending on the assessment you choose. Each test is designed to be detailed enough to generate meaningful insights while remaining manageable for a student to complete comfortably.

17. Can my Psychometric Test result change over time?

Your interests, experiences, skills and understanding of yourself can develop as you grow. A psychometric assessment captures your profile at a particular point in your journey, so your results should be viewed as a useful reflection of where you are today rather than a permanent label.

18. Does a high score mean I will definitely succeed in that career?

No. A test result cannot guarantee success in any career. It can highlight areas of alignment and possibilities worth exploring, while your effort, skills, learning, experiences, choices and circumstances will also play an important role in your journey.

19. What if my test results don't match the career I already want?

That does not automatically mean your career choice is wrong. Your results can give you another perspective to consider and may help you understand what attracts you to that career, where you may need to develop further and what related possibilities you could explore.

20. What should I do after taking the Psychometric Test?

The test is only the beginning of the exploration process. You will have the opportunity to understand your report, explore the suggested possibilities, ask questions and experiment with different career ideas before making important decisions about your future.
